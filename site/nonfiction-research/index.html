Codex · Open-source writing studio

Nonfiction research and citations for books

Keep research, sources and citations connected to your manuscript in an open-source writing studio.

Codex is in development and runs on your own infrastructure. Check the requirements before installing.

Build a knowledge base as you research

Record people, ideas and source material in entries you can return to while writing. Use categories that fit your project and connect relevant entries to the manuscript.

Track sources and quoted passages

Add sources in References and cite them in scenes. Codex tracks uses and quoted-word counts against the limits you set for the book. The clearance workflow records permission requests and replies and identifies risks that block print export.

These checks support your workflow; they do not determine copyright law or replace legal advice. You remain responsible for assessing quotations and obtaining permission.

Prepare the book for print

Review citations and clearance before exporting a print-ready PDF. Printing requires a compatible host; see the requirements.
