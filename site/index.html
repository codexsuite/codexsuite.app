Why Codex

A book is more than a document.

Long non-fiction lives across outlines, notes, citations, permissions and print files. Codex keeps all of it attached to the manuscript, so nothing drifts out of date.

Structure you can see

Acts, chapters and scenes on a board. Move a scene and the manuscript follows. Label what needs research and see at a glance what's left to write.

Research beside the draft

People, laws, studies and stories live in a knowledge base that links itself into your text. Click a name to open everything you know about it.

From draft to printer

Every quote checked against publisher rules, a bibliography built for you, and a print-ready PDF from real LaTeX templates when you're done.

FAQ

Questions from writers.

What is Codex?

Codex is a free, open-source writing studio for long books and series. It puts book planning, a research knowledge base, a manuscript editor, citations and copyright clearance, and print layout in one app, so you can go from outline to print-ready PDF without moving your book between tools.

Can I write fiction or a novel in Codex?

Yes. Codex is designed around non-fiction first, but the same tools work for novels and fiction series. Plan acts, chapters and scenes on a board, keep characters, places and backstory in the knowledge base under your own categories, draft from scene beats, and lay the book out with the built-in Novel template at 6×9 or 5.5×8.5 inches.

Is Codex an alternative to Scrivener?

Codex covers the drafting and structure you might use Scrivener for, and adds what usually takes separate apps: a knowledge base that links itself into your text (a story bible, if you write fiction), source and permission tracking, and print layout of the kind you would otherwise do in a formatting tool such as Vellum or Atticus. It is free and open source.

Can I use Codex to self-publish a book?

Codex exports a print-ready PDF from LaTeX templates, with your choice of trim size, bleed and crop marks when your printer wants them, and a preflight check for low-resolution images. That is the kind of file print-on-demand services such as Amazon KDP and IngramSpark ask for.

Does Codex have an AI writing assistant?

Yes. Write a short scene beat and Codex drafts 200 to 600 words from it, using your plan and knowledge base as context so the draft fits the book. You choose the model, and you can regenerate or edit every word.

How much does Codex cost, and when can I use it?

Codex is free and open source. The designs are done and version 1 is being built now. Follow the project on GitHub to see it take shape and to hear first when early access opens.

Is Codex related to OpenAI Codex?

No. Codex at codexsuite.app is an independent, open-source writing studio for authors. It is not affiliated with OpenAI or its Codex coding agent.
