Codex · Open-source writing studio

Book outlining for chapters and scenes

Plan a book in acts, chapters and scenes, then keep the outline beside your manuscript as you write.

Codex is in development and runs on your own infrastructure. Check the requirements before installing.

Give your book a structure

Use the Plan board to arrange a book into acts, chapters and scenes. Break a large manuscript into smaller pieces, give scenes a purpose and revisit the order as the book develops.

Move from outline to draft

Open a scene in Write to work on its prose with its summary nearby. Scene beats provide prompts for drafting; you choose what to keep and edit the result. The outline remains part of the book as the manuscript grows.

Keep the research close

Use the knowledge base for people, ideas and sources alongside the draft. For longer projects, a series can group related books and share settings and style guidance.

See the guide to structuring a book and the drafting guide for the workflow.
